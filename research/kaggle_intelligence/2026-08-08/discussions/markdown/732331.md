As the Simulation Category enters its final stage, we would like to provide an overview of what comes next.

The teams advancing to Second Round  will be determined based on the results of the Strategy Category in First Round.

Following the conclusion of the Strategy Category, the top eight teams will advance to Second Round and compete in a tournament to determine the champion. If any qualifying team withdraws, the next highest-ranked team will be invited as a replacement.

Second Round is scheduled to be held as an in-person event in Tokyo.

# Qualification for Second Round
Teams aiming to advance from the Simulation Category to Second Round must also participate in the Strategy Category using the same team.

Please refer to the following page for the evaluation criteria:
https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/pokemon-tcg-ai-battle-challenge-strategy/overview/evaluation

While your ranking in the Simulation Category will be taken into consideration, submissions will be evaluated holistically based on additional factors, including deck construction, the originality of the proposed approach, and the quality of the explanations provided in the report.

# Second Round Environment
Second Round will introduce the following changes from First Round:
- Each match will be played in a best-of-three format (BO3). 
- Teams must use the same deck and the same code throughout the match, and the first team to win two games will win the match. 
- The games will be played sequentially rather than simultaneously. 
  - Therefore, teams will be able to access the log from Game 1 during Game 2, and the logs from Games 1 and 2 during Game 3.

# Submission Resources
- AWS p5.4xlarge (or equivalent) 
- NVIDIA H100 GPU (80 GB VRAM) 
- 256 GiB RAM 
- 16 vCPUs

# time limitation
- Each team will have 30 minutes of total thinking time per game. 
- The time limit is separate for each game in a BO3 match and cannot be carried over from one game to the next. 
- If a team runs out of time, it will lose that game.

# Card List
- All cards used in First Round will remain available in Second Round.
- Second Round will be played with an expanded card pool that includes additional cards. 
  - Details of the additional cards are scheduled to be announced after the final rankings for the Simulation Category have been confirmed.

# Additional Information
- For more information about Second Round, please also visit the official event website:
https://ptcg-abc.pokemon.co.jp/
- More details—including the additional cards and other tournament logistics—will be shared in future announcements.

We hope you continue enjoying the Simulation Category, and we look forward to seeing many of you in Second Round!
