Sharing my getting-started notes as a one-page visual summary (in Japanese) — the rules, the constraints that actually shape strategy, and a rough roadmap.
![](https://storage.googleapis.com/kaggle-forum-message-attachments/3474035/45676/file_000000009b6472099186dc94ca32ca37.png)
Quick gloss of the main points.  
• You submit two coupled things: a policy (main.py) + a deck (deck.csv).   
• No ingress/egress during episodes → the agent must be self-contained (no live LLM calls).   
• The engine hands you the legal moves each turn, so a first working agent is easier than it looks.   
• Win/loss TrueSkill, latest 2 subs count → consistency beats blowouts.

How are you approaching it? Net-decking a known Standard list or building from scratch? Heuristics + search, imitation from the exported top episodes, or full self-play RL? What's your approach?
