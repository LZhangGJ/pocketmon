Hello Pokemon Trainers,

It was very entertaining to see the meta develop over the first few days - Abomasnow on top with an incredible amount of Lucario (probably due to the example Lucario that was provided), then Crustle, then Typhlosion that beat Crustle (sometimes a mixture of the two), then Starmie that could beat either Crustle or Typhlosion, then Alakazam, and now it's still been mostly Crustle and Alakazam. TR Mewtwo, Grimmsnarl, Garchomp, and now Festival Lead have showed up in the top tiers as well but are not nearly as prolific.

My questions for y'all - **How have you picked what deck to use in the competition? Have you made changes over time? Are people picking a deck based on the meta, on certain results, something else?**

I'm currently training agents on self-play, with a different agent for each popular archetype (Archetypes are arbitrarily grouped by me based on whether the deck included or excluded certain pokemon e.g. Alakazam decks include Alakazam and Dudunsparce). The agent trains against every other popular archetype, and then I keep the best agent based on it's win rate against every popular archetype after a training session (wins/losses into an archetype are weighted based on how popular they are in the replay data).
I then create my own deck(s) to test locally against the agent(s).

I'm hoping to create a deck that can get past the starting 600 elo.. I'm incredibly new and naive to machine learning, and I'm learning heavily on domain knowledge of the PTCG to close the gap. I think once I can establish that my agents are actually learning and improving, I can create a more focused deck that is made to counter Kaggle's specific meta (acknowledging that 10% of the time I will face a completely random opponent).

Your answers, feedback, critique, questions are all welcome. This is my first competition and I'm having a blast learning.
