## 2026-06-17 snapshot

From the public Game History I checked yesterday, the visible top-10 meta looked heavily concentrated around sustain / wall-style strategies.

### Top-10 archetype mix

- Crustle-style sustain / wall / deck-out pressure: 10 / 10
- Hybrid sustain with a backup attacker: present, but still within the broader Crustle-style family
- Fast tempo / energy acceleration: not clearly represented in the visible top-10
- Psychic-style control: not clearly represented in the visible top-10

### Practical takeaway

At that point, my main evaluation axis would have been:

1. Sustain / wall / deck-out pressure
2. Long-game resource management
3. Backup attacker plans behind a defensive core

## 2026-06-18 snapshot

The visible top-10 meta seems to have shifted a lot compared with the previous snapshot.

### Top-10 archetype mix

- Iono-style lightning tempo: 3 / 10
- Psychic-style control: 2 / 10
- Crustle sustain / hybrid: 2 / 10
- Other grass / fire / spread hybrid decks: 3 / 10

### Change from previous snapshot

- Increased: lightning tempo and psychic control
- Decreased: pure Crustle-style sustain concentration
- Stable: Crustle hybrid strategies are still present, but no longer appear to dominate the visible top-10 alone
- New practical concern: evaluating only against sustain / wall decks is probably not enough anymore

### Practical takeaway

For local evaluation, I would now test against these broad axes:

1. Fast tempo / energy acceleration
2. Psychic-style control
3. Sustain / wall / deck-out pressure
4. Hybrid midrange with a backup attacker

## Summary

The main lesson for me is that this competition may have a fast-moving meta even in the public portion of the leaderboard.

I would be careful about overfitting to one visible archetype. A stronger evaluation suite probably needs several broad opponent families rather than only the current rank-1 style.
