I put together a short checklist for making Pokémon TCG AI Battle submissions more reliable:

https://www.kaggle.com/code/koki1231/ptcg-ai-battle-reliable-submission-checklist

It focuses on practical deployment issues I ran into while testing agents locally and on Kaggle:
- `main.py` may be loaded without `__file__`
- lazy imports inside `agent()` can fail if `sys.path` changes after load
- local tests should mimic the harness more closely than a normal `import main`
- episode `remainingOverageTime` traces can help confirm whether expensive code actually ran
- every agent should have a minimal legal fallback

The notebook does not include any competitive strategy or agent-specific logic; it is meant as submission hygiene for anyone building their own agent.
