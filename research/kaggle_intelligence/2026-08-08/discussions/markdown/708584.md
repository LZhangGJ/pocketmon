We are excited to launch this competition in collaboration with Kaggle and The Pokémon Company. This challenge focuses on developing AI Training Agents that are capable of playing the Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) in a competitive environment.

As a strategic game of its own, Pokémon TCG players must make gameplay decisions while being mindful of their opponent's own strategies, decks and hands. When formulating their approach to the game, players need to take into consideration various Pokémon types and thousands of different card combinations to consider various gameplay possibilities. In addition, other factors such as card draws and coin tosses introduce additional gameplay variables.

In this competition, participants will train AI Training Agents that will compete against one another within a digital Pokémon TCG simulation environment. Participants will be tasked with developing an AI Training Agent.

Some key aspects of the competition include:
- Consideration of an opponent’s unknown hand and strategies
- Simulation-based AI Training Agent training and evaluation
- A combined Simulation Category and Strategy Category competition structure that encourages both performance and research insights.

The Pokémon TCG AI Battle Challenge will be open to interested AI developers and researchers, engineers and students from around the world, including those less familiar with the Pokémon TCG.

If you're new to Kaggle competitions, we recommend starting with the Getting Started section and the starter kit provided with the competition.
