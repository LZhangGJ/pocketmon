  I have experimented with behavior cloning and rollout-based plan selection, but so far their offline improvements have not transferred reliably to the live leaderboard. I’m wondering whether higher-rated agents—especially those around 1000–1100—are mainly based on:

  - Hand-written heuristics
  - Search or simulation
  - Behavior cloning / imitation learning
  - Reinforcement learning or self-play

  1. Your approximate rating range
  2. Whether your method performs consistently online
  3. Whether BC or RL actually improved over a strong heuristic baseline

  5. Whether matchup-specific routing has been useful

  Thanks! I’d love to understand what approaches are working for others and whether strong heuristics are still  competitive with learned policies.
