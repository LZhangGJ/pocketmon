## Message 3475126 — unknown

Posted: 2026-06-19T12:12:12.553Z

In-person or online games of the Pokémon TCG are absolutely fine datasets to use. If you can create the dataset yourself, it's generally considered acceptable. If you were to manually play a number of TCG games with friends, record the results, and use that to train your solution, that is completely fine. However, if you were to purchase a large dataset of games online that costs money that would not be considered allowed.

You do not need to team up with the person you are playing the game with, but don't use it as a loophole to discuss tactics in *this* competition.
