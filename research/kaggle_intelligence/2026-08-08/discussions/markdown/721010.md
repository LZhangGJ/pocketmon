# Summary

| Deck                        | Core Feature                                                   | Main Strength                | Main Weakness                     |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Dragapult**               | Bench pressure with `Phantom Dive` damage spread               | Strong board control         | Needs Stage 2 setup               |
| **Dragapult / Dusknoir**    | Adds `Cursed Blast` for forced KO math                         | High KO potential            | Prize/risk management is hard     |
| **Dragapult / Blaziken**    | Blaziken ex accelerates Energy from discard                    | Faster attacking tempo       | More setup pieces                 |
| **Slowking**                | Copies attacks from non-Rule Box Pokémon via deck-top control  | Flexible attack options      | Requires careful sequencing       |
| **Ogerpon / Meganium**      | Grass Energy acceleration and Energy doubling                  | Strong mid-game scaling      | Evolution-heavy setup             |
| **Raging Bolt / Ogerpon**   | Discards Energy for big one-hit damage                         | Explosive damage output      | Burns resources quickly           |
| **Mega Venusaur / Ogerpon** | Moves Grass Energy and heals while attacking                   | Durable late-game board      | Slow setup                        |
| **Crustle**                 | Blocks attack damage from opponent’s Pokémon ex                | Strong anti-ex matchup       | Weak to non-ex answers            |
| **Mega Lucario**            | Direct Fighting-type damage with Energy acceleration           | Simple high-damage plan      | Predictable attacks               |
| **Mega Lucario / Hariyama** | Adds gust pressure through Hariyama evolution                  | Can target key Bench Pokémon | More complex setup                |
| **Alakazam / Dudunsparce**  | Builds large hand size for `Powerful Hand` damage              | Strong draw engine           | Damage drops if hand is disrupted |
| **Starmie / Froslass**      | Spreads damage counters and pressures Bench                    | Good chip-damage control     | Slow against fast decks           |
| **Marnie’s Grimmsnarl ex**  | Attaches many Darkness Energy to Marnie’s Pokémon              | Big Energy acceleration      | Depends on setup/stadium support  |
| **Kangaskhan / Bouffalant** | Basic Pokémon durability with Bouffalant damage reduction      | Tanky and simple             | Lacks strong finishing power      |
| **Mega Absol Box**          | Toolbox deck with many situational attackers                   | Very flexible                | Hard to pilot consistently        |
