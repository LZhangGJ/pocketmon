First off the daily top episodes datasets are great, thanks for putting those together. They are super handy for seeing strong play and pulling training data.

One thing they do not quite cover though. The daily sets are the top episodes across the whole comp, not my specific agent's games. When I am debugging my own bot I want all of its matches, wins and losses, not the global best ones. A lot of my losses are exactly the games that would never make a top episodes set, and those are the ones I learn the most from.

Right now to get those I open each match on my agent's page and grab the replay one by one. Fine for a couple, kind of painful at twenty.

Could we get a button at the top of an agent's match list that just zips up all of its replays and downloads them together? Episode id filenames like they already are so parsing scripts still work. A wins only or losses only toggle would be a nice bonus since losses are usually what you want, but the plain grab everything button already fixes the main annoyance.

Should be cheap since the replays already exist and already download one at a time, so it is just zipping files you are already serving. Cap it at the last N games if history size is a worry.

Basically the daily datasets cover "show me good play," this would cover "show me my play." Both useful, different jobs.
