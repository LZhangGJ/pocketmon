#### Message 3477944 — unknown

Posted: 2026-06-22T06:56:35.633Z

Thank you for the thoughtful comment.

We understand that a single-deck setup can create incentives for counter strategies, especially near the top of the leaderboard. We also recognize that the Kaggle metagame may shift quickly as participants respond to the decks and strategies that are currently performing well. For example, if a certain deck or strategy becomes common or successful, other participants may try to build decks or strategies that are strong against it.

At the same time, we see this kind of metagame awareness and adaptation as part of the strategic challenge of Pokémon TCG. In this competition, we expect participants to choose and build a deck while considering the likely metagame, and then compete through robust in-game decision-making with that chosen deck.

The evaluation is not based on a single match only. Since submissions continue to play multiple matches over the evaluation period, targeting one specific opponent or one short-lived metagame should not be enough on its own. Strong agents will need to perform consistently across repeated matches and different opponents.

For that reason, our expectation remains that each submitted agent uses a single deck consistently.
