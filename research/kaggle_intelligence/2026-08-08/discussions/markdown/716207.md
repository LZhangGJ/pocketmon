I wanted to share some insights and failed experiments I’ve had while trying to build an anti-meta deck, and see if anyone here has found a solution.

### My Findings on Archaludon and Ogerpon
To defeat the dominant Archaludon decks, I discovered that Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex is the perfect counter. Because Archaludon relies on an Ability, Ogerpon's natural immunity completely walls it. We can easily take down Archaludon with this strategy.

The issue is when we fight against Starmie or Lucario decks. Pure Ogerpon decks suffer greatly in these matchups because they just get out-sped or hit through their defenses. To survive in this meta, we clearly need a good combo deck to counter the other popular decks. 

### My Hybrid Deck Experiments
I have tried building a few different hybrid combinations to cover Ogerpon's weaknesses:

1. **Ogerpon + Miraidon (and other Electric types):** I tried pairing Ogerpon with Electric attackers to counter Starmie. While it has great potential on paper because of the weakness advantage, it failed in testing because Electric attackers are too slow to power up compared to how fast Starmie attacks. 
2. **Ogerpon + Archaludon:** I also tried mixing Ogerpon with Archaludon itself to make a wall deck. This failed because trying to run two completely different energy types caused the deck to be too inconsistent, resulting in a lot of dead hands.

I have attached all the Ogerpon decks I have built or collected so far to this post so you can see exactly what I was working with.

It feels like an Ogerpon combo deck is the key to disrupting the meta, but the energy acceleration and consistency issues are holding it back. 

**Have any of you tried running Ogerpon with Electric-type decks or found a combo that actually works against Starmie and Lucario?** If you have any deck ideas or insights on how to solve this, please share them!
