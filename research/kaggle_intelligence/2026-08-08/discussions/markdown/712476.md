Hey there,

Yes, a re-submitted identical agent may take time to climb back and may settle at a slightly different score due to matchmaking randomness but given enough games, it will converge to approximately the same rating. The leaderboard reflects skill, not submission order. However, that's all else equal - it's also likely that newly made submissions are stronger and adapting to the strengths on the leaderboard, so you'll face better submissions early on (as opposed to seeing your high-ranking submission slowly erode over time).

At the end of the competition we don't reset scores, but instead extend the amount of time that games are played until we are more confident. 

Games run continuously every few minutes, the system picks which submissions need games most (new bots and uncertain ratings first) and matches them against similarly-rated opponents.
