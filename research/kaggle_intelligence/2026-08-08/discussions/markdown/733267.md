When text under an attack says, "...this attack does damage...," then it is something Shaymin can block (Grimmsnarl Ex, Wellspring Mask Ogerpon Ex). Otherwise, what you are reading is normally 'an effect of an attack' (Dragapult Ex Phantom Dive, Munkidori Mind Bend).
Battle Cage can stop opponent Munkidori's Adrena-brain and Dragapult's Phantom Dive on your bench. It doesn't stop Adrena-Brain to your active. If you own Froslass on your bench, it will still place damage counters **on your own** pokemon with abilities in between turns, because Froslass is not, per Battle Cage, "your opponent's attacks or abilities."

| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Battle Cage | Damage counters being placed on your Benched Pokémon by the effects of your opponent’s attacks or Abilities | Damage dealt directly by attacks                           |
| Shaymin     | **Damage** dealt by your opponent’s attacks                                                                 | Effects of attacks or Abilities that place damage counters |
