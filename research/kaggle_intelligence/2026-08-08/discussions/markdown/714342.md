## How I Would Read These Signals

I would not treat any of these as proven strong agents just because the title contains a high score or because the Notebook has votes. Those are useful signals, but they are still only signals.

For public analysis, I would first ask:

- Does the Notebook explanation match the public output?
- Is the result understandable without relying only on the title?
- Is the same archetype or idea appearing from multiple sources?
- Does the idea conflict with any active Discussion about simulation behavior or scoring?

The Starmie theme is the most interesting to me right now because it appears in more than one public place. The Strong Start / Psychic signals are also worth watching, but I would be careful not to overread title scores before checking the public output.
