I think that step count is good for 3M model unless you plan on scaling to 50M steps or so. I would suggest checking what the true botteleneck is beceause the env is fast already most of the wasted time is on the NN. If you want to write the engine for full gpu compatibility, it should be considered due to the nature of this comp. Almost every attack carries effect text, and the real monster isn't the vocabulary of effects —a couple dozen primitives cover nearly everything — it's composition: effects chain in ordered sequences, and abilities/trainers are mostly triggered or passive logic. Doing that properly means building a vectorizable effect/trigger system, not just a transition function. For orbit wars that required compelex aim, planet position etc. calculations during the rollouts especially with forward simulations which made it necessary for full gpu transition. One side note is that if you have tokens just leaving it to an agent for a long session and saying make sure its byte identical might work haven't tried though.
