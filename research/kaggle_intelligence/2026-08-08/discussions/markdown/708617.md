## Takeaways

- **Lucario and Dragapult are the top tier, but ~50/50 head-to-head** — Lucario only leads overall by beating Iono harder (78% vs 64%).
- Clear rock-paper-scissors element: every deck loses to the field but **Iono crushes Abomasnow 74%**.
- First-player win rate is **51.5%** — a small but real going-first edge.

Numbers are from the *sample* agents as-is, so they reflect deck + baseline policy together, not deck ceiling. Curious if others see the same ordering.
