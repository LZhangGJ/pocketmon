The [Overview page](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/pokemon-tcg-ai-battle) links to the Pokémon TCG's Rules & Resources on pokemon.com, an English website.
However, the [English rulebook](https://www.pokemon.com/static-assets/content-assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/rulebook/meg_rulebook_en.pdf) is a fairly light rulebook that sets up the game but does not address the underlying logic, and doesn't do anything to help players understand the templated language convention of the game. This makes it difficult to train an AI to understand particular types of interactions between cards.

The main website of the Pokémon Card Game, pokemon-card.com, has a significantly more extensive manual that is located on [https://www.pokemon-card.com/assets/document/advanced_manual.pdf](https://www.pokemon-card.com/assets/document/advanced_manual.pdf) and provided a fantastic framework to train AI to understand the Pokémon card game. *(An English version for the East Asian markets is also available; [https://asia.pokemon-card.com/ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2025/10/EN_advanced_manual-2025.pdf](https://asia.pokemon-card.com/ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2025/10/EN_advanced_manual-2025.pdf))*

The difference in the breadth and comprehensiveness of these rulebooks makes it easy to anticipate disparity in rulings. From conversation with players and past familiarity with the game, in official organized play, there is a history of English players being provided rationale for gameplay interactions that is different or sometimes entirely contradictory from that provided to Japanese players. *(Examples can be provided.)*


In the event of any gameplay interaction that does not resolve identically across the two languages, what would be the resolution process?

Thank you for raising this, and apologies for the delayed response.

The game engine used in this competition has been implemented based on the Japanese Pokémon Card Game rules.

If there are detailed differences in rulings or interpretations between the Japanese and English rulebooks, the competition engine follows the Japanese rules and interpretations.

That said, if there are any differences between the behavior of the actual Pokémon Card Game and the behavior of this game engine, the behavior of the game engine used in this competition (PTCGABC) will be treated as authoritative for the purposes of this competition.

If you find any specific interaction that appears inconsistent or unclear, please feel free to share the details, and we will review it.
