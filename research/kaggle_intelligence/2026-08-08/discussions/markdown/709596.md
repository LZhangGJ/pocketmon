When in doubt, share it publicly in the competition forums.

Sharing something like "Hey I'm considering a rules-based approach and I've got 10 years experience with Python, would you like to team up?" is not private sharing. 

Sharing something like "Hey here is my notebook, take a look. If you like it, let's team up" is private sharing.

What constitutes private sharing? The competition rules strictly prohibit sharing code and data privately, but it is not clear if this extends to high-level discussions about approaches (programming languages, rules-based modeling vs reinforcement learning, building agents to play specific decks vs a more generalized agent, etc).

This is particularly important during the process of constructing teams, where alignment on which approaches to initially prioritize and general codebase structure matters (ie: messages to friends who are potential teammate candidates).
