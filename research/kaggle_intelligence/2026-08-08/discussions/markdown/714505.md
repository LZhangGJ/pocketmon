Through this PTCC Competition, I became interested in Pokémon cards.

Recently, a mini tournament was held at a Pokémon card shop (Korea), so I decided to visit.

On the way to the card shop, I came across many interesting items.

One of them was a decorative object whose purpose I had been curious about for a long time.   
I wondered if it was something for the kitchen, like a dispenser for dish soap or shampoo, or perhaps a pencil holder.

Even after discussing it with countless people, no one could figure out what it was actually used for.
