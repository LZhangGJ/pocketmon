The card description returned by `cg.api.all_card_data()` for **Team Rocket’s Great Ball (card ID 1132 / DRI 175)** is incorrect.
`all_card_data()` currently says:
>Search your deck for a Trainer card, reveal it, and put it into your hand. Then, shuffle your deck.

However, the official card effect is:
>Flip a coin. If heads, search your deck for an Evolution Team Rocket’s Pokémon, reveal it, and put it into your hand. If tails, search your deck for a Basic Team Rocket’s Pokémon, reveal it, and put it into your hand. Then, shuffle your deck.

The game engine already implements the official coin-flip effect correctly. I reproduced both branches with fixed seeds: one offered only Basic Team Rocket’s Pokémon and the other offered only Evolution Team Rocket’s Pokémon.
Therefore, this appears to be a problem with the text exported by `all_card_data()`, rather than an engine implementation bug. This can materially affect agents and deck-building tools that rely on `all_card_data()` as the authoritative card description.
Could you please update the `all_card_data()` entry—and any supplied competition CSV containing the same incorrect text—to match the official card?
