We are very happy to see that so many of you are already enjoying the competition!

We encourage you to consider participating in this competition as a team.

This challenge requires not only skills in Kaggle competitions, machine learning, reinforcement learning and systems engineering, but also domain knowledge of Pokémon TCG and TCGs in general, including deck building and battle strategy.

For example, we believe that teaming up can be especially valuable for participants such as:
- Those who are strong in Kaggle competitions or machine learning, but are not yet familiar with Pokémon TCG
- Those who have deep knowledge of Pokémon TCG or TCGs, but are not yet familiar with Kaggle

By collaborating with participants from different backgrounds, we hope to see a wider variety of interesting approaches and stronger submissions.

That said, there are several important points to keep in mind regarding team mergers. Please make sure to review the following before forming or merging teams:

- Once you join a team or merge teams, you cannot leave that team.
- The maximum team size for this competition is 5 members.
- In this competition, the Simulation Competition and the Strategy Competition use the same team composition.
- The submission limit is shared by the entire team. In this competition, each team can submit up to 5 times per day.
- Team mergers must be completed by the Team Merger Deadline.
- Please make sure that all team members understand and follow the competition rules.
- Teams are collectively responsible for their submissions, code, deck.csv, and Strategy Competition materials.

When looking for teammates, it may be helpful to include information such as:

- What you can contribute
  - Examples: Kaggle, machine learning, reinforcement learning, systems engineering, Pokémon TCG, deck building, analysis, etc.

We hope that Kaggle experts, Pokémon TCG experts, and participants who are learning both will use this Topic to find great teammates and enjoy the competition even more.

Please feel free to use this Topic to introduce yourself and look for teammates.

Good luck, and have fun!
